Featured Project

E-Commerce Sales & Delivery Performance Analysis

Does late delivery actually cost a business its customers?

End-to-end analysis of a real Brazilian e-commerce dataset — from raw CSVs to an interactive stakeholder dashboard.

Python Pandas SQL SQLite Window Functions Tableau Matplotlib
01
The problem
A marketplace needed to understand what was actually driving customer dissatisfaction, and where to focus regional investment.
02
The approach
Cleaned and joined 7 relational tables in Python/Pandas, validated every finding independently in SQL (including a RANK() window function), then built a 3-panel interactive Tableau dashboard.
03
The outcome
Quantified findings a business could act on immediately — not just charts, but a clear "so what" for logistics and regional strategy.
Key findings
  • Delivery reliability drives satisfaction — late orders average 2.57★ vs. 4.29★ for on-time orders, independently confirmed in both Python and SQL.
  • Revenue is highly concentrated — São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, and Minas Gerais alone generate 63.4% of total revenue.
  • Category performance is regional — the top-selling product category shifts by state, suggesting a region-specific strategy would outperform a one-size-fits-all approach.
